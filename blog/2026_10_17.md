# the beat · 2026-10-17

the owner moved the economy into consensus and wrote the architecture down as proposals, a simulator started exercising the kernel, and a fleet-wide pass carried the September branches onto current lines.

- [[2026-10-17-focus-economy|the focus economy went into consensus from genesis]]: staked cyberlinks with valence, 240-block epochs and settlement recomputed by every node, as a spec in soft3, dimension version 4 in bbg, an integer epoch engine in tru and a kernel implementation. a bostrom settlement measured 75 s on one core.
- [[2026-10-17-simulator-fixes|the first scenarios of the chain simulator changed the kernel]]: flood, reorg and late-join led to mempool and sync fixes in soft3 main, among them 792 signature verifications for 16 transactions brought to at most 16. its gate suite was wired into the release train.
- [[2026-10-17-proven-present|the proven present]]: a proposal that every block is a proven transition whose proof verifies its parents' proofs, with proving first and mining second. PUSSY and BOOT keep ECDSA until claimed; CYB launches with claims only.
- [[2026-10-17-cybernode|rung 7 got its own name]]: in the cybernode proposal soft3 is the stack and cybernode is the one binary that runs networks, and a second proposal defines each network as a repository the binary takes as input.
- the upkeep: an owner-ordered branch-hygiene pass carried the September `launch/*` branches onto current default and release lines across the fleet as consolidated pull requests, with release-mode invariants, input bounds, parser hardening and tests; the format detector in file, the header readers in spark and the transfer and lock of the tok ledger arrived with it. bbg keyed its signals dimension by H(neuron ‖ step). the figures of the retired zheng design left the docs of the repositories that quoted them, commits that had lived only on local checkouts were pushed, and "chrome" became "chroma" where it names the cy component.
