# cyberia.to put a sky behind its home: planets on Kepler elements under the stars of Yale BSC5

five commits rebuilt the home of cyberia.to, the site of [[cyberia]].

the triad tiles gained their targets: the space tile points at cyberia.my, the money tile at cyb.money, and the thesis reads uniting space, money and knowledge. the first sentence of the tagline stays on one line, a pause sits between it and the next so that a reader can take it in, and the coda has a line of its own: flourish together, then on the way to the stars. the tiles carry a left saber where a box stood, and the anthem blurs only under itself.

behind the desk layout runs a living solar system: a corona, orbits, a rotating earth with a moon, saturn with rings. it is hidden on a phone and stands still when asked. the last commit of the day made that sky a real one. the planets follow Standish/JPL Kepler elements, which the commit marks as the choice for now, the camera pins the sun on the left and the earth on the right, and the stars of Yale BSC5 pass through the same sky, looking along the milky way.
