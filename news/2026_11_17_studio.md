# the cyberlink composer moves into cybergraph as studio

cybergraph #26 carved the cyberlink composer out of cyberia.my into a new directory, `studio/`, and changed nothing else in the repository. studio is its own trunk + leptos (CSR) package with an empty `[workspace]`, so the root crate builds without it, and the base path is read at build time from `STUDIO_BASE` so the app can be served under a sub-path. a contained preview of it is at https://cybergraph.link/pr/studio/.

the studio speaks the vocabulary of the book: neuron, particle, cyberlink, signal, intent. a link is the five stored fields from, to, coin, amount and valence, carried in a signal under its subject. what moved from cyberia-my is the neuron (a mudra domain key), the particles (hemera), the draft signal, and commit and verify, where commit is an ADR-036 signature over the canonical body. the pages became one composer screen, a signal page and a signals list. cyberia.my's word/rel/float-weight model, the seven-button bar, the ladder strip, the KPI tiles and the TSP labels stayed behind.

signals and intents are signed and kept locally, under `cg.studio.*` in localStorage; nothing is submitted to a chain, and every face of the app says so. a second commit added the acceptance walk: `walk.py` drives two links, a commit, re-verification, a reload, a tampered signal, the lists, an intent, a file particle and a check for horizontal scroll, and `serve.py` serves `dist/` under a base path so reloads on client routes are checked too.
