# studio: the cyberlink composer became an app of its own in cybergraph

the cyberlink composer left cyberia-my and became studio, a standalone app in a new directory of [[cybergraph]], with a preview at cybergraph.link/pr/studio. what moved: the neuron as a mudra domain key, particles as hemera hashes, the draft signal, its commit as a signature over the canonical body, and its verification. the link and signal pages were rewritten as one composer screen, a signal page, a signals list and an intents page.

on the way the studio took the words of the spec. a link is the five stored fields of `specs/cyberlink.md`: from, to, coin, amount and valence. the `rel` word and the float weight are gone, and the amount is a whole number. a particle is the hemera hash of a file's bytes: typed text is hashed as a file, a pasted 64-hex string is taken as a particle, and a chosen or dropped file is hashed in the page. `me` names the neuron's own particle. a signal carries each link under its subject, and the canonical body signs the subject with the link.
