# the lease agreement and its annexes open in fifteen languages

the leasehold pages of [[cyber-valley]] gained an edition per language through the day. after Indonesian and English came Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and Hindi, and the last commit counts all fifteen editions on the eight pages. each language opens beside Indonesian. the agreement was aligned with its signable edition, every page names its PDF editions, and every land and company term names its scan.

the library of [[cyberia-my]] put the same fourteen second languages on its shelf, with one ASK row for any other language. English now leads every document title with Indonesian beneath, the covers were re-cut to match, and the agreement reads «Lease Agreement» in English and «Perjanjian Hak Sewa» in Indonesian. the content hash on a document page became a QR of the document's IPFS address. the papers that arrive before signing open the page as their own dossier.
