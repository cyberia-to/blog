# every TOCYB traced from the 2021 genesis to the halt and to CYB

an audit page in [[cyber]] follows the 1,000,000,000,000,000 TOCYB of the [[bostrom]] genesis of 2021 through its allocations to the halt, and states what each becomes at the cyber genesis: 1 TOCYB unit is 666 cy, and 10¹² cy is 1 CYB.

the 2021 allocation was cybergift 70.0 %, cybercongress 11.6 %, the epizode zero community 8.3 % across 555 genesis accounts, the senate (the community pool) 5.1 % and the great web foundation 5.0 %. the v6 upgrade, applied at height 20,667,000 on 2025-09-26, burned 718,594,467,532,355 units: 603 T from the gift multisig and 115,594,467,532,355 from the congress multisig. ten days earlier, on 2025-09-16, the great web foundation multisig had moved its 50 T to the congress key, an account separate from the congress multisig. the gift multisig kept 97 T because the burn was a fixed 603 T, sized to the BOOT it still held.

at the halt the supply is 281,405,532,467,645 TOCYB over 1,434 holders, and the top 20 hold 93.6 %. that supply becomes 187,416.08 CYB at the cyber genesis: 88,962.41 CYB (47.5 %) live, 98,453.67 CYB (52.5 %) dormant in the gift multisig, the community pool and the IBC escrows. the conservation check closes to the unit against the snapshot supply.

the page was assembled in bostrom/audit through four pull requests and moved the same day to cyber/audit/genesis.
