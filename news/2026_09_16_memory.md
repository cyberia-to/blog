# memory, a world written in rune

the memory world of [[cyb]] is now a page: `cells/memory.rune`. it is the first world authored in [[rune]] rather than in Rust. the page queries particles, bytes, links and rows through the host, and [[prysm]] draws the census row.

that needed things rune did not have this morning: stat and row chunk-nouns, a table made of rows (the `tr` noun was killed the same hour it was born), a header that is one row even when the body arrives as a list, columns that are tracks rather than suggestions, cells that are one line with overflow fading into the black. the table shows name, focus, size and when — with when compacted to now / 3m / 2h / 5d / 8w / 1y.

underneath, cyb now stands on a particle. identity and openers live in two small crates, `particle` and `spark`; tapping memory or brain stands on the thing instead of opening a graph overlay. there is no close. you switch world or stand elsewhere.
