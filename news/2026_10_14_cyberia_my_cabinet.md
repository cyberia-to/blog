# cyberia.my gained a holder's cabinet, a document library and a page for every flat

eleven commits extended cyberia.my, the application of [[cyberia]].

the plot page gained survey and media cards: they show the ground's data from /plot-data, or an ORDER button that opens Telegram. a bot turns those orders into tasks in the crew's group, pins the results to IPFS and publishes them to plot-data. the side column of the page follows the status of the plot, and real data is shown with no mock badge.

every flat got one crawlable page with a title, a description, JSON-LD, a plan.svg and ERC-721 metadata, and the site got a sitemap and robots. the holder got a room at /cabinet with statuses, a neuron, an Ethereum wallet, KYC and support, and a visitor whose browser has no wallet is shown how to get one. a document library opened at /docs: the documents are stored on IPFS with their CIDs in the library, and the signable pack is served as PDFs on the letterhead, one per instrument and one for the whole pack.

a payment step tracks what was sent against what was received, a register of three statuses, reserved, transfer and owned, paints the map, and /admin serves the valley and the notary. the landing carries the [[lytics]] tracker.
