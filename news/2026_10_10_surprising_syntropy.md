# surprising syntropy: one short page for what the CYB mint pays

[[cyber]] gained a short page, `surprising syntropy`, for the one quantity the $CYB mint pays: order the network gained that the crowd did not already expect.

the page takes the two words as two filters. syntropy asks whether the shared picture got clearer: it is the sharpness of focus over the cybergraph in bits, and the part of its move that a cyberlink caused, counted only when it is downhill, is that link's impulse, Δφ⁺. surprise asks whether anyone needed the neuron to make it clearer: every contribution carries a Bayesian Truth Serum report, the neuron's belief and its prediction of what everyone else believes, and the serum squashes the score to a weight ρ between 0 and 1. a copy or a link the crowd already predicted goes to 0, a true link the crowd under-predicted goes to 1.

the mint weighs every contributed link by its surprise before it measures the shift, then splits the result among the contributors by their Shapley shares. the page draws three consequences: copying earns nothing, idle capital earns nothing, noise earns nothing. a passive stake has no report to surprise anyone with, so staking earns from the mint as an active bet.

the page is linked from the litepaper, the whitepaper, rewards, staking, impulse and tokens, and [[tru]] links it from §3 of its reward spec and from its syntropy term.
