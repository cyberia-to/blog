# soft3.org: the ladder became the front page, with a chart of every component and a loop

soft3.org was rebuilt in twenty-three pull requests (#95 to #117) into three pages that share one chrome. the front page is the ten-rung ladder. rungs 1 to 7 are the products of the stack, each wearing one of the seven colours of the flag in order: nox form, trident mass, joy space, cybergraph life, neural word, cy work, [[soft3]] play. rungs 8 to 10 are the apps, [[cyb]], [[cyber]] and [[cyberia]], which open their own sites (cyb.ai, cyber.page, cyberia.to) and are shown without parts. hovering a rung previews its colour, a click pins it, and Escape clears it. on screens up to 900px wide the ladder flows as a plain list.

the chart puts the apps at the centre, seven hubs around them and every registry component on one outer ring, grouped by product in ladder order. clicking a rung on the front page opens the chart on that product. the `/layers` page was retired in #95, the registry was updated to match, and `check-components.py` checks the ladder and the chart.

the third page is the loop, three verbs on one circle: LINK (a mind: cybergraph, neural, cy), PROVE (the machine: trident, nox, joy) and FOCUS (the network: tru, foculus, soft3), with the focus becoming the next thought. `/scheme` redirects to `/loop`. the last merges of the day set one shell width of 1400 for all three pages.
