# one proof: the repair of zheng got its goal, 64 KB, post-quantum, 1 ms, constant size

[[soft3]] took a proposal, `proposals/proof-system-repair.md`, in eight pull requests merged within an hour. the goal, fixed by the owner on 2026-10-09: a proof of at most 64 KB, post-quantum, verified in at most 1 ms, constant in size. the proposal records the measured state of the art, the holes behind the old 2 KB claim with their evidence, and a design for [[zheng]]: one field with fp3 challenges, one hash, a Reed–Solomon code, Spartan over CCS, ARC accumulation, and one wire format with public, succinct and zk profiles. the first byte budget estimated 32–40 KB for one hash at 128 bits and 56–70 KB at 2^20. the work is cut into five phases, each with its acceptance.

the review rounds of the same day changed three things. the commitment stayed open: phase 2 became a bake-off between the existing TensorMerkle with a Reed–Solomon code and WHIR, run on full prototypes, zk included, with bytes accounted per component and verification timed on first contact and with a cache. the goal split into two size classes, one system with two openings behind one trait: small statements, gated at 20 KB with 16 KB as the stretch, and unbounded computation at 64 KB. the lattice schemes were read as well: they buy a homomorphic accumulator at the price of a second assumption, and a Neo-style spike was scheduled before phase 3.

the landing page of soft3 was changed with it: the proofs feature states the goal and the measured numbers in place of 2 KB.
