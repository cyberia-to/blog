# a proposal prices the writing of state and leaves the holding free

a proposal landed on [[soft3]] main as `proposals/state-economics.md`, under the line "price the act, free the being, fund the machine forever". it turns §3e of steps-blocks-epochs into a spec.

it separates two products. writing state is energy, paid once at the margin. holding state is persistence, free forever: the proposal carries no rent, no dormancy, no eviction and zero mortality terms.

four rules and one curve carry it. a net-new slot is priced W0 × M(phi) on a 1-2-4-8 occupancy ladder, with W0 anchored to the block reward. the price is payable as a burn for ephemeral state, or as a lock, a reservation that unlocks on delete, which the commit names the no-death mechanism for long-term agents. an expansion valve opens at sustained 75 %. security is funded by one asymptotic curve, supply(h) = C(1 − q^h) with C at most the field size: an eternal budget with a provable cap.

the commit names its survey, nervos' two-layer structure and parano1d's pricing, a six-number complexity budget, and the questions it leaves open: per-dimension phi, the lock k, a rescaled expansion window for 1 s steps, and the machine share.
