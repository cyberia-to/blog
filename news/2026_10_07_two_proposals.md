# two proposals on soft3: tade as the one exchange format, and the network cut in planes

[[soft3]] gained two proposals, both with the status proposed, and an index page that lists every proposal with its status and one line.

the first makes [[tade]] the one format data takes on every boundary of the stack: the wire, the payload of a link inside a signal, a command line's machine channel, the durable log and the stream a painter reads. it divides the labour among four components: the nox noun computes, a tade frame carries, spark shows and prysm draws, and the structural payload of a frame is a canonical noun. six steps follow, among them a canon with conformance vectors, proofs that own their bytes (the page counts 12 postcard call sites in zheng and 6 in lens), and command lines that print ANSI on a terminal and a tade stream when piped. the sixth step was done the same day: tade's chip moved from rung 7 to rung 4 of the ladder, beside file and signal.

the second reads the network as planes: reach and carry in [[radio]], frame in tade, then sync (reconcile), [[foculus]] (agree) and solong (reward), which the proposal counts as three planes inside one foculus crate of 13k lines. the plan is three crates in one workspace with one arrow of dependency, sync below foculus below solong, in the same repository. radio stays one repository; the line between reach and carry becomes explicit, and the transport-independent cores of the reconciliation engines leave it for sync. rung 7 of the ladder was updated to read radio · sync · foculus · solong.
