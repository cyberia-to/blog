# soft3.org got a landing page: thirty features, each with its claim, its source and a figure

the front page of [[soft3]]'s site became the product in eighteen pull requests (#139 to #156), and the previous home, the pitch and the ladder, moved to `/layers`. the hero reads: collective, private, provable computing — compute once. prove it. everyone remembers. below it the features stand in a list on the left and the chosen one opens on the right with its claim, why it holds, the parts, the source and a figure. each feature has a deep link, `?f=<id>`.

the list began at twelve and ended the day at thirty. the first set ran from frozen foundations and post-quantum through private, memoized and sovereign to interplanetary. names, fast finality and trinity made fourteen, and cross-verified and trusting trust sixteen. eight more came from a stack audit, each grounded in a spec, among them collective focus, graph not ledger, useful mining and provable reads. rewards split into truth staking and resource markets, and the last additions were location proofs, home books, referral, native silicon and the wire.

the compact feature carries a measured figure. counted with tokei 14 on 2026-10-08, code lines only, tests and data files excluded, the stack is 275k lines, 425k with radio, set function by function against the usual parts: 3.2M lines before LLVM and 38.8M with it. #143 draws that as three bars on one linear scale, ×7.6 and ×91.

several cards state their own status: the trinity card says which uses are open, the fast finality card marks its parameters as illustrative, and the resource markets card marks relay as proposed.
