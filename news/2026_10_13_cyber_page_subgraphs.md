# cyber.page mounts the public subgraphs again, and the litepaper names what inflation buys

the publish workflow of [[cyber]] had dropped the subgraph clone in the protocol-only cut of 2026-09-07, and 2,655 links on cyber's own pages, 42 of them in the litepaper, rendered as yellow stubs. the site is again the protocol pages at the root with the public census, the blog repository's `subgraphs.toml`, mounted beside them. siblings contribute markdown only, since code and data pages would be 1.4 GB, and the deploy moved to the netlify-cli digest method for 14k files and 441 MB. the workflow gates on zero unresolved links in the litepaper. it fired that day on a link to a spec that existed only as uncommitted work, and the sentence was removed.

the litepaper changed in six pull requests. a paragraph states that inflation buys order in three forms: truth from stakers, fairness from settlement miners, order in time from folders and the beacon. a short chapter, truth · fairness · order, holds the organizing idea of the incentives. the milestone table became one inline SVG on a log scale, from self to Avogadro. the best money was rewritten as a matrix of safe, easy and smart against gold, the dollar, bitcoin, ether and zcash/monero, with the unit: 1 CYB = 10¹² cy, a cap of 18,446,744 CYB and a genesis of 187,416 CYB at 666 cy per TOCYB. apps became a chapter, and the roadmap reads past · present · future, where the present named one focus: the return of [[bostrom]] and space pussy on [[soft3]] on 2026-11-05 with the same keys and balances.

[[optica]] made table cells wrap and made tables and display math contain their overflow. the litepaper had measured a scrollWidth of 1771 at a viewport of 1440 and the whitepaper 2699; after the change both sit at exactly the viewport width.
