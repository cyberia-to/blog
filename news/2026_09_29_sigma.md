# sigma got its own repository: a token registry and three measurements, with cx as the default measure

sigma, the organ of cy that shows the sum of what a neuron holds, moved into a repository of its own with a contract, `specs/sigma.md`, in draft status. the contract has two parts. the registry is the owner's list of tokens, fungible or otherwise: each entry names the token's particle, its kind (coin or card), its unit, and one method for each of three measurements. the measurements are price (what one unit is worth in another token), balance (how much a neuron holds) and supply (how much exists), each taken of a token, on a neuron, at a moment. a method is a rule that produces the number. the standard set includes `book`, `market`, `formula`, `chain`, `conservation`, `fixed` and `hand`, and sigma reports every value together with the tier it came from.

from the three measurements the page derives the rest: the value of a holding, the sum over the registry, a neuron's share of a token, capitalisation, and the price of any token in any other. the default numeraire is cx, the century index, a fixed basket of eight world assets. a second commit the same day made cx a formula token present in every registry from birth, and the owner may set any other token as the measure.

the [[soft3]] registry was pointed at the new repository in pull request #78, with the ladder unchanged. the surfaces the spec names are `cy sigma` in a terminal and the sigma world in [[cyb]].
