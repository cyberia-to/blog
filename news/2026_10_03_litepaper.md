# the cyber litepaper was rewritten around one law: money minted for a proven reduction in collective uncertainty

the [[cyber]] litepaper was rewritten in a run of commits, and the whitepaper followed with the same idea. the new opening states the thesis in one line: cyber mints money for a proven reduction in collective uncertainty, paid to whoever sharpened the shared picture. the page calls it one law with two readings, a superintelligence in one direction and a money of finite supply in the other.

the centre of the text is a table of four questions: what is the state, who sharpened it, was it honest, and who pays for the first three. the answers are one focus identical on every node, each contributor's Shapley share, the Bayesian truth serum with the surprisingly-popular signal, and one security budget split between work and stake by the active staking ratio. the page reads the four as one computation and names the last, the hybrid of work and stake, as cyber's own, the other three being borrowed theorems.

the [[soft3]] section draws the ten layers as an inline diagram. the roadmap sets three networks in one table, BOOT on [[bostrom]], PUSSY on space pussy and CYB on cyber, with bostrom's graph at burial given as 2,949,732 links, 3,143,650 particles and 61,675 accounts. four phases follow, from foundations to the CYB mainnet.

the same day added an audit of the game-theoretic foundations (four pillars, the seam, six results to apply), linked from the foot of the litepaper, and restored 33 sentence subjects in the whitepaper.
