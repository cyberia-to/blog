# cyberia.my gets an OPEN NOW list and a three-step checkout

three pull requests changed cyberia.my on one stated focus: selling the leasehold. #31 turned the flat page's LAND board into a PLAN: the flat drawn to scale, north up, every side signed in metres, with area, perimeter and span in the heading. a side shorter than its own sign is left bare so a many-sided flat stays readable. the map's YOUR REQUESTS list and the dock's request counter were removed, and the passport dropped the pruned cells and placed figures together with their dead code and CSS.

#32 added choosing and buying in one line. the map's city panel opens with OPEN NOW, every leasable flat cheapest first, and one press selects it. pressing LEASE had written a request into the browser and painted the flat IN TRANSFER on arrival; the flat is now requested when the buyer presses I HAVE SENT. the checkout reads as three numbered steps (how you pay, where you send it, tell the valley), followed by what happens next: confirm, agreement and survey, signed. the yearly rent is shown for the first time.

#30 reduced the top bar's pill to a single red marker, renamed from MOCK to PROTOTYPE, by one CSS rule site-wide. the flat page's real pill is unchanged.
