# the metabolic oracle moved from cyberia's architecture into the core

a proposal page in [[soft3]], the metabolic oracle, takes the mechanism out of the architecture of [[cyberia]] and places it in the core, on the owner's word of 2026-10-23 that the oracle, without the agents, belongs there.

the page fixes the picture. three signs, cap, syntropy and happiness, form an epoch record in consensus. M is a weighted geometric mean in fixed point. Ṁ is the governor of parametrization and of the emission floor. the weights are the one stake vote. at genesis the case is degenerate, M = J, so the launch carries nothing new. the page also lists what is cut from the cyberia version, the acceptance vectors and the order of work, and it names the homes of the substance: rewards.md §16, the record and the vote in chain-economy.md, and the privacy rule set for the private reports.

the paired change in cyberia rewrote the section of foundation/architecture. it keeps its table and its formula and points at the soft3 proposal, and the agents became readers of M and voters. both pull requests merged into the main branch of their repository.
