# cyberia/fathership: the way of working written as a roadmap

[[cyberia]] gained a page named fathership. the launch page of [[cyber]] says what ships; this page says how: the way the owner governs the work of agents, and the road from a hand-driven process to development that runs on the [[cybergraph]]. its thesis is that the owner sets the vision, breaks the ties agents cannot break and promotes releases, while research, planning, implementation, review, merging within policy and cutting candidates are the agents' work, done in the open and with evidence. three numbers measure the stance: the share of merges with no owner action, the time from a task becoming ready to its change merged, and the decisions brought to the owner together with how many were real ties.

the first commit laid out three phases (a scaffold, a dev dialect on the cybergraph, the graph as the only truth), a property registry of 25 rows, and a council and pool across four vendors. later commits the same day added properties 26 to 28 for the second phase, in which the patch is the wire and GitHub a mirror, restructured the page into seven subsystems with three diagrams, and added the canon in four layers together with a goals page for the quarter.

the dev page, the law every agent works by, was reorganized into the loop, conduct and house rules. one decision recorded on the page was carried out at once: the vision page moved from cyberia's foundation to cyber valley.
