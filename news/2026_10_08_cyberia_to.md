# cyberia.to became one page: the thesis, nine tiles, an anthem that keeps playing, and files for agents

cyberia.to was rebuilt across thirty-two commits in one day and ended as a single page. it began the day as two pages, a front page with the thesis, the symbols and the anthem, and a `/projects/` page with the projects and the set. later in the day the projects moved into the graph as the constellation page of [[cyberia]], every project with a door on the web, and the navigation went away.

the page holds three things. the brand sits centred on top and the thesis types itself on arrival; its wording settled through the day on a cyberstate as a shared mind uniting space, capital and knowledge, for humans, machines, animals and all life. nine tiles stand three by three, ordered as in the thesis with space, capital and knowledge first, and carry the marks of the projects redrawn as 40×40 vectors with slow animations that hold still under prefers-reduced-motion. the anthem, Cyberia Calling, plays from a persistent bar with a download button, and a deliberate pause lasts for the session.

the page also speaks to agents: llms.txt, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, JSON-LD in the head and a social card. first paint needs one html file and two fonts, with the stylesheet inline and the Play font self-hosted, and every vertical size scales with the viewport height so that the page fits one screen on a phone or a desk.

the home page of the cyberia subgraph was rewritten the same day with the same thesis: the cyberstate, led by superintelligence.
