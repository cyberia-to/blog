# cyberia.my becomes a globe: one map from the planet to the plot

the cyberia-my home became a globe in a day. #5 wrote the project page, with the owner's brief: a vector globe, detailed inside down to districts, a plain satellite bitmap, the land console at the deepest zoom, everything served from our own servers. the page measured 952,093 units in geoBoundaries gbOpen and excluded GADM by licence. #6 built it: `src/globe.rs`, a WebGL2 engine in the app (Rust to wasm, web-sys, no map library), one camera over a sphere from the planet to 2 m across, cities as markers, fly-to, and an address for every view, so a link opens the same view.

the rest of the day refined it. #7 added held keys that ease in and out, read by key position so they work on any keyboard layout. #8 drew the administrative divisions from grid cells and added search by name, with a static index by the first three letters. #11 and #12 made the top bar two places of one map, EARTH and CYBER VALLEY, flying the camera without a page reload. #14 made the descent to the city fade in instead of cutting, #15 made the map the whole page on a phone with a bottom sheet for the selection, #16 redrew the cabinet page, and #13 moved the audio player to the middle of the header.

#17 added a layer tree on the L key: earth, borders and cyberia groups, with the choice kept in the browser. #18 deepened the imagery, so the world's land descends to zoom 11 (76 m a pixel) from Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX, CC BY 4.0. the cyberia-my pull requests were checked on previews at https://cyberia.my/pr/globe/.
