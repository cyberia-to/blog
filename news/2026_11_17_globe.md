# cyberia.my's home became a globe: one map from the planet to a plot

on the owner's word of 2026-11-17 the home of cyberia.my became a globe, and the project page, the engine and the data merged within the day. the engine is WebGL2 inside the app, Rust compiled to wasm with no map library: one camera over a sphere from the planet down to 2 m across, imagery tiles draped on the sphere, cities as markers, and the address of a view is the view itself. the land console and the globe are one map. the engine is a layer under the console's drawing and owns the only camera, so plots, districts, trails and the panels work over the imagery.

the divisions of the world are drawn, searched by name and reach the village: 1,040,641 units, with Indonesia carried down to 7,069 kecamatan and 81,912 desa from geoBoundaries gbHumanitarian. a tap gives the point and every level it is in. the imagery is Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX under CC BY 4.0. outside Bali and Armenia the world had stopped at zoom 7, 1.2 km a pixel; the fetch now descends by content to zoom 11, 76 m a pixel, and leaves out the source's blank tiles. a layers tree names what the map shows, and on a phone the map is the page.
