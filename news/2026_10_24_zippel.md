# zheng's IOP written as zippel protocols: complete as written, a planted bug caught

two pull requests to [[zheng]] took up the owner's question, whether the IOP proves what it claims.

the first records an assessment of eniac/zippel (UPenn, S&P 2027) as a checker for zheng's IOPs. the gains it names: derive the transcript instead of maintaining it, and use the completeness and special-soundness analyses as the oracle for agent-written protocols. the form it names: an analysis-only Goldilocks backend, `.zippel` specs beside the markdown and a CI job on a nightly toolchain. zippel's place is the checker and never the runtime, since it covers curves and pairings only, with no hash-based PCS and no GPU.

the second ran the experiment. the IOP was written as zippel choreographies in specs/proto in five variants: whole, fixed CCS, outer, inner, and one with a deliberate bug. the verdict is in `audit/zippel-2026-10-24.md`. the IOP is complete as written, analysed in 43 ms. the outer and inner sumchecks pass alone up to 3 and 4 variables. the planted bug, a degree-3 round polynomial on three points, type-checks and runs, then fails completeness in 4.5 s. the derived transcript order matches `transcript.rs`. special soundness does not apply in the oracle model: it needs the commitment inside the protocol.

the README carries the run recipe, with zippel as a separate nightly checkout that stays outside the dependencies.
