# the referral spec: a referrer share that decays from 10% to a 1% floor at Dunbar scale, opened by a witness

[[cyber]] gained `specs/referral.md`. a bind-once referrer earns a share of every settled reward of its referee, taken from the 10% pool that li already reserves. for a referrer with n direct referees active within a window of 30 epochs the share follows a hyperbola, s(n) = max(F, P·D / (D + 9n)): it starts at the pool, P = 10%, and reaches the floor, F = 1%, at D = 150, Dunbar's number, which the page marks as an anthropological estimate. beyond 150 the share stays at the floor. the referrer cut of a settled reward rounds down, and the referee keeps the rest.

the curve is hyperbolic so that income n·s(n) rises with every referee; the page notes that a linear descent to the floor would peak near n = 83 and then pay less for each newcomer. past D growth continues by delegation, with new people heading their own branches.

the second part is the witness gate. two fresh identities at 75 referees each would earn about 1.82× what one identity earns at 150, so the curve opens only to a witnessed identity, one nullifier for one curve, and an unwitnessed referrer earns the flat floor at any n. the page names the payout point, `apply_settle_receipt` in [[cyb]] core, where the referrer cut is credited with a matching mint leg.

the same day added the pages the spec links to: dunbar number, sybil-proof and cyb-boot.
