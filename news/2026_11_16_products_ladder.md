# the products page became a ladder of ten rungs, each product with its door

cyberia/products was rewritten through the day as one table: a rung, a product, the page that is its door. [[soft3]] is the stack, the aggregate of the seven products of rungs 1 to 7, drawn above them: nox the machine, trident the weapon, joy the warrior, cybergraph the brain, neural the language, cy the agent, cybernode the network. [[cyb]], the robot, is rung 8. rung 9 is [[cyber]], the superintelligence, with three tokens under it: $CYB at cyb.money, $PUSSY at spacepussy.ai and $BOOT at bostrom.network. rung 10 is [[cyberia]], the state, with what the state keeps: the game aos, the city cyber valley with its districts, the space management console shown as my cyberia, the century index cx, the operations apparatus, the cyberstates network and the capital shown as $CAP.

five doors were named that day: nox.band, joy.technology, cybergraph.link, neural.how and cy.army. cybernode's door is cybernode.ai alone, and soft3.org is the stack's. the page keeps its rule: every claim on a product page carries a dot, measured, target or mock, and a product is delivered when every dot is green. at the end of the day every row carried a dash in the dots column, the mark of a page with no marked claims yet.
