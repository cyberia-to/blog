# each document of the cyberia.my library opens through its own page, and the leasehold shelf holds one instrument

the document library of [[cyberia-my]] changed shape over the day. Bahasa Indonesia became the text of every document: a second language opens beside it or is asked for, and the reading pack left the shelf. a document now opens through its own page, which shows the cover, what the document is and the language it opens in. the cards on the shelf carry the title alone, in Bahasa Indonesia with English beneath.

the leasehold shelf was reduced to one instrument, the agreement with Annexes A–G. the simple form and the notary's template were retired; earlier the same day the notary's template and the signable pack had opened in English beside Indonesian.

the leasehold pages of [[cyber-valley]] took the same decision in one commit: one instrument, the agreement is the akta, and the simple form and the notary's template are retired there too.
