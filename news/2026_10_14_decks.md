# cyber.page, cyb.ai and cyberia.meme became decks on one engine, and soft3.org's deck is served at new.soft3.org

the home of cyber.page, the site of [[cyber]], changed from optica's index into an interactive deck of seven chapters: superintelligence, anatomy, focus, compile, syntropy, frozen, network of moneys. it is vanilla html, css and js with no build step, and every graph page keeps its URL. the visitor links two things, iterates the tri-kernel, mines a settlement, stakes on a claim and makes a mentor link, and a HUD keeps the yield. figures carry a mark for measured, target or mock. the graph on screen is a sample of the sealed [[bostrom]] graph, 1,200 particles and 6,000 links. a `fit()` pass keeps each slide inside the viewport, verified on 12 viewports from 360×640 to 2560×1440.

the same engine went to three more sites. cyb.ai, the site of [[cyb]], became a product deck of seven chapters and fifteen slides, with screenshots of v0.16.0, the twenty-one parts with their status words, downloads per platform, and a track with a play toggle in the HUD. cyberia.meme, which its first commit calls the meme state, opened with a canon wall and a meme maker that mints a png with a sha-256 particle, then took seven chapters on the engine with the cybergraph behind every slide.

soft3.org, the site of [[soft3]], became a deck of 60 slides with the feature cards moved to /features/, then one deck of 67 slides that carries the product story, the world and the charts. by the owner's call the home, /layers, /loop, /chart and /scheme returned exactly as they were, and the deck is served at new.soft3.org until it is judged ready.
