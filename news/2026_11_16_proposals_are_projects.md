# there are no proposals, only projects: one registry collects every repository

the word proposal left the fleet. on the owner's decision of 2026-11-16 the folder `proposals/` in [[soft3]] became `projects/`, with its history kept and every link rewritten, and the folder's README states the status rule: proposed (a draft), accepted (a project), executed, rejected or superseded. soft3's part merged into `release/phase1`. foculus renamed its own folder, and eight sibling pull requests rewrote the links in cyber, bbg, evy, hemera, mudra, nox, strata and zheng, docs only.

the registry at cyberia/projects states what a project is: finite, with a delivery, a date and a declaration, the page its link opens. the order of the table is the priority, and the first row is worked first. the table fills from the bottom: a collector reads `projects/*.md` of every repository under cyberia-to, on its default branch and on the release line `release/phase1`, every 15 minutes, and every page has a row whatever its status. the same day the table gained a product column that names the product each project serves. what is kept alive and measured by revenue and capacity is a product and lives on the products page.
