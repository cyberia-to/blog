# radio repaired its BAO path: canonical root, 64-byte parent pairs, a workspace that builds and tests

five pull requests in two days fixed the BAO path of [[radio]], and a sixth wrote down where the transport goes.

the proofs: valid right-only BAO proofs could fail with `Truncated`, because the walk over excluded descendants popped the hash a pending right subtree expected; the walker keeps it. the widths: a parent pair is two 32-byte hemera values, 64 bytes. the exporter's three output methods and the import paths of MemStore and FsStore copied a 32-byte value into a 64-byte half and panicked; one encoder keeps the writers and the reader consistent.

the root: a complete file spanning several 4096-byte chunks inside one BAO group received an internal chaining value as its key. a shared reducer applies ROOT on the final node across producers and verifiers. identities change for lengths of 4097 to 65536 bytes on the native group size; existing keys, tickets and the collections that refer to them are reconstructed from complete bodies.

the workspace: `cargo test --locked` at the root compiles and passes everything except three mDNS tests that failed on the test machine for an environmental reason. iroh-docs and iroh-bench build on the fork, iroh-blobs runs on 32-byte hemera hashes and 4 KiB chunks, and `Hash::EMPTY` is the tree root of the empty input. optimizing hemera alone in the dev profile took the iroh-blobs library suite from 337 s to 41 s.

the roadmap read the 194K-line tree against the specs: about 5.3K lines are the project's own, and the module built the soft3 way is projected at about 14K own lines, transmit only, in nine gated steps.
