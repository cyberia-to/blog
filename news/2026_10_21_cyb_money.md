# cyb.money gained a launch block, a comparison of five kinds of money and a genesis checker

twenty commits to [[cyb]] reworked the cyb.money landing in one day. navigation became one flat sequence of states: every arrow, wheel and swipe moves one state forward or back, and the first and last states disable the arrows.

get CYB became a launch block with two pages, the chains and then what a holder gets at genesis. its order is the chains first, then claims, then transfers.

the smart chapter is a horizontal comparison of five kinds of money, each group with icons and one rounded order of value, the groups ordered from the largest to the smallest: production (gold, silver), debt (USD, CNY, EUR), energy (BTC, XMR, ZEC), capital (ether and solana standing for the proof-of-stake field) and information (CYB, with PUSSY and BOOT beside it).

commit ddfafbe put the cap on the page, 18,446,744 CYB, with its genesis share: 187,416 CYB, 1 % of the cap, to TOCYB holders at ×666. the cap and an interactive emission curve ended the day on the scarce chapter, the curve drawn discrete by year and called illustrative by the commit that added it. the genesis chapter carries a checker by passport name, address, mnemonic, private key or ledger. it reads the bostrom burial snapshot, derives keys locally and shows the CYB a holder gets at the genesis, with 1 CYB = 10^12 cy. BOOT and PUSSY stay outside the conversion.
