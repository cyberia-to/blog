# soft3 builds on Intel macs, and the miner's auto backend measures WebGPU before it mines on it

the Intel-mac leg of the [[soft3]] release train stopped at the release build: the GPU crate of [[lens]] compiled its Metal backend on every mac, and that backend reaches [[honeycrisp]] `acpu` 0.2.0, drivers for Apple Silicon, which gave 9 errors on `x86_64-apple-darwin`. lens #41, merged into `release/0.2`, gates the Metal backend on `all(target_os = "macos", target_arch = "aarch64")` across 13 cfg sites, and soft3 #328, merged into `release/phase1`, does the same for the 5 metal cfgs of the miner. an Intel mac builds as Linux does: metal answers "not built" and webgpu remains. in a later rehearsal the Intel leg built the release in 15m 26s and node-status reported `version: 0.10.0`, `height: 0`.

the build exposed the next stop. `soft3 mine --backend auto` picked WebGPU whenever an adapter opened, and on the `macos-15-intel` runner the adapter "Apple Paravirtual device (Metal)" never returned from a 2^22-nonce dispatch: the bench timed out after 600.1 s, while the CPU mined at 72.4 kH/s in debug. the `macos-14` runner reports the same adapter name and does 480 kH/s, so the name cannot tell the machines apart.

soft3 #353, merged into `release/phase1`, makes Auto measure. WebGPU gets a probe at its own dispatch size on a detached thread with a 5 s deadline, while the CPU searches 2^14 nonces of the same job. WebGPU is kept only if it answers in time, agrees with the CPU on the least passing index and hashes faster per second; otherwise Auto prints the reason on stderr and mines on the CPU. Metal under Auto and every explicit `--backend` stay as they were. on the Intel runner the full `cargo test --locked` then exited 0 in 388.0 s, with `mine_e2e` at 27.88 s.
