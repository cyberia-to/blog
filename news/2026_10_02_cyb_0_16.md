# cyb 0.16.0: chat on its own page, soma that writes as it thinks, five binaries

[[cyb]] 0.16.0 was published on 2026-10-02 as a cut focused on the interface. its notes open with a warning: the pussy chaosnet is not expected to work in this release.

conversation moved to its own page, apart from the log. a question typed anywhere hops to chat, with the question on the left and the answer on the right, while `ls` and pipes still run as the shell. soma answers live: the reply bubble opens at once, the prompt pass shows a count such as `reading 120 / 380`, and tokens append as they land. the kernel side of that is in cy, where `poll` began to carry `Prefill {done,total}` during the prompt pass and a `Delta` per new character during decode.

on Android the app's home is its internal files, so the graph and the identity survive, and weights pushed over adb are copied off FUSE before mmap. the log became a windowed table of cyberlinks (step, from, to, token, stake, valence), and memory is the same table ranked by focus. sigma paints the last known balance while the chain fetch runs, and the referral share of settle rewards is in.

the release carries five binaries and a SHA256SUMS file: a dmg for Apple silicon, an Android apk, a Linux x86_64 tarball, a Windows x86_64 zip, and an unsigned iOS ipa for TrollStore or jailbroken devices.
