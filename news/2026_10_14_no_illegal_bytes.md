# soft3 settled its stance on content: information is legal, there are no illegal bytes

status.md of [[soft3]] keeps a list of seven questions that no roadmap in the stack owns. the fourth was titled deletion and liability: an append-only content-addressed graph will be handed illegal bytes, right-to-be-forgotten stands against content addressing and moderation against censorship, and the page called it the hardest political question, currently unasked.

the item was replaced by a stance, which the page calls settled. there are no illegal bytes. an append-only content-addressed graph is handed every kind of byte and keeps all of them, and the page names that the design. the protocol has no censor verb, no tombstone and no right to be forgotten against content addressing. a node holds and serves what it chooses, a local choice and never a protocol rule. focus decides what is seen. liability belongs to whoever acts on information, never to the record.

what remains is engineering, and the page hands it to the next item of the list, storage economics: replication that follows focus, so that unloved bytes fade with nobody deleting them. the soft3.org deck changed with the page: the line of its three-constitutions figure that read deletion policy with an owner reads information is legal, written down.
