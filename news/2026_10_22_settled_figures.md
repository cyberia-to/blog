# the lease template's figures settled, and a page gives the origin of each

the lease agreement on the [[cyber-valley]] pages lost the brackets around its figures. three commits settled the terms that had stood as bracketed values: the completion security, the holding charge, the buy-back and limitation periods, the density fee, the service charge cap, the initiative and the processing fee among them. the stay management fee was set for now, as a provisional value. the grant is stated as being for mixed purposes, and clause 11.3 gained a rule for fewer than ten Holders.

a new page, value provenance, gives the origin of every figure of the lease, written for the buyer. two further pages, company licences and office practice, are the two pages the lease links to.

[[cyberia-my]] re-pinned all fifteen editions with the settled figures and placed a reading-translation note under every document's language list: the Indonesian text binds.
