# valkirya, the school of empresses of cyberia, published a call for a director

the [[cyberia]] graph gained the page valkirya. it reads: the school of empresses of cyberia opens soon, a school for women who want to lead, and until then the school is looking for a director. the vacancy became a page of its own, valkirya/director, which the school page links, and every spelling of the name was set as an alias of the school page.

cyberia.my serves the vacancy at /valkiria/director in English, Russian and Armenian, styled with [[prysm]], each language with its own link and link preview. the terms of the intro link to their graph pages on cyberia.blog, Bali links to the map and cyber valley to cybervalley.io. a looping video follows the intro, and The Call plays on loop through the cyberia player.

the graph page lists the work in three lines: run the company in Yerevan, keep the rhythm of the school, and connect Yerevan and cyberia. Armenian and English are required. the steps to apply are on the page.
